# Halal guardrails (AJ: non-negotiable)
- No interest and no late fees on customer credit. Vendor credit price equals the cash price (no extra for delay).
- Write a cheque only if the cash will be in the account when it is presented. Date honestly, no backdating, stay within the agreed due date. The vendor payable stays until the cheque clears.
- No hidden charges. Descriptions and ads are honest: give equal or slightly more than paid for (the 100.1 rule).
- If unsure whether something is halal, say so and ask AJ. Never decide it silently.
