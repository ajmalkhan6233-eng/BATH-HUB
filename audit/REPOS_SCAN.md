## 2. BATHCO: what is in it
**History.** 1 Jun 2026: first Python/FastAPI prototype (`software/`, abandoned). 16 to 22 Jun: BATHCO COMMAND phase 1 (daily entry, Excel parsing, reconciliation check, 34 tests, golden-core tag, OCR of handwritten expense sheets, QA audit). 23 Jun to 5 Jul: Railway prep, Nature frontend, feature flags, white-label config, LAYLA v1. 6 to 27 Jul: LAYLA fixes, June closed, the AZMI Rs 8M question resolved (a personal investment, not a loss), then the "ledger-fixes" programme (27 items). The repo stops on 27 Jul; the live system and BATH-HUB moved on after that.
**Versus BATH-HUB.** BATH-HUB is a superset of the old server: about 390 routes against 172. Only 6 old paths do not exist in BATH-HUB: daily-entry sync (3), cheque-tracking (2), and `/api/audit/day-invoices`. Everything else (accounting, payroll, attendance, VAT, quotations, nl-query, TOTP...) is already there, usually newer.

**The big prize that is NOT in BATH-HUB:**
1. **Unmerged branch `origin/ledger-fixes-2026-07-25`** (16 commits): `public/daily-entry-v2.html`, an offline-first daily ledger (Sales, Expenses and Payouts, Cheques, Range tabs, keyboard entry, pinned totals, undo, print, local backup) with multi-device sync (`routes/daily_entry_sync.js`), a **cheque clearing-date calculator that skips weekends and Sri Lankan bank holidays** (2026 list included), and a **Lasersoft PDF parser** that reconciles typed sales against the POS and shows PENDING instead of guessing profit. It was built with NO LOGIN by design, so it must sit behind login first. Do not merge the branch blindly: it deletes CLAUDE.md and PROGRESS.md; cherry-pick the code files.
2. **Real business data, in git history (not the current tree)**: 232 spreadsheet files (commit `a1ab8f7^`, folders `DALI/` and `data/`): about 60 nightly daily-sales sheets, Lasersoft invoice and profit exports (about 3,200 and 3,900 rows, Oct 2025 to Apr 2026), product master with cost, price, stock (about 2,600 items), purchases (about 3,250 GRN lines), a physical stock count of 2026-04-20 (378 variance lines, 793 uncounted), the outbound cheque ledger (252 cleared cheques, Rs 114M), a vendor list (about 130 vendors with balances), and tile catalogue PDFs/HTML (up to 16 MB each). In the current tree: `MASTER_LEDGER/master_ledger.xlsx` (192 days, 21 Dec 2025 to 30 Jun 2026; 100 days still carry the placeholder Rs 50,000 expense), 43 OCR'd expense sheets, 5 JSON backups (suppliers 24, staff 13, supplier payments 47, credit customers, salary).
3. **Scripts worth porting** (BATH-HUB lacks them): product cost/stock import from the Lasersoft price list (`import_product_costs.js`), item-level margin import with honest ESTIMATE flag (`import_item_level_sales.js`), OCR aggregation rules with a unit test (`ocr_expense_photos.py`, `import_ocr_expenses.js`), a receipt-by-receipt day reconciliation (`reconcile_bathco.py`), stock analytics (fast movers, non-moving, items sold below cost, count variance in `software/*.py`), the per-invoice day audit query, and an AES-256-GCM single-file vault backup (`backup_vault.py`; BATH-HUB already has an equivalent).
4. **Business rules** (summarised in `bathco_docs_data.md` section 4): data-truth hierarchy (Excel day sheet = revenue truth, handwritten sheet = expenses and payments, Lasersoft = GP only); day formulas (cash out = expenses + payments, cash in hand = cash sales - cash out, net profit = GP - expenses, float Rs 25,000, legacy salary/cash_out columns must stay 0); the honesty rule (no number without a source: show PENDING, never 0); manual-bill and after-hours rules; checker suppression list; supplier cleanup (AZMI never merged, brand names are not suppliers); data tiers FULL/CASHFLOW/FOUNDATION.
5. **The 2026-07-20 testing-safety incident** (CLAUDE.md section 17): automated tests ran against today's real date and a cleanup `DELETE ... CURRENT_DATE` wiped a whole real day. BATH-HUB has the same daily-entry style of page: copy the rule (tests only on a fake date or a throwaway database) into AI_MEMORY/LESSONS.
6. **Insights** (`BATHCO_CONSOLIDATED_REPORT.txt`): tiles about 57% of sales but the lowest margin (about 12.5%), taps and accessories 45 to 65%, 53 item codes with stock and zero sales, Rs 22.3M of sales in items missing from the stock list.

**Not worth taking:** the 93 one-line `claude-commands/` stubs, `software/` FastAPI app and Docker "AI empire" (abandoned), `bathco-mobile` (paused read-only Expo viewer, 60-70% done; BATH-HUB is already an installable app), `schema.sql` (stale), the GLM export documents (duplicates), n8n flows, `credit_customers` files (look like seed or demo data, do not import).
