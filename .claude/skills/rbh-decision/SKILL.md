# Decision brief
At most 3 options, the recommended one first marked (Recommended). One line each: what it gives, cost in usage or money, time. The exact answer to paste in a code block, yes or no where possible. Small decisions: take the recommended one and tell AJ. Big ones (money, live data, publish, delete): wait for his word.
