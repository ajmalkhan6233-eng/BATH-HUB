# Price learner (AJ prices by feel, 2x to 3x cost; cheaper items get a bigger multiple)
Start from docs/AJ_LEARNED.md. Median multiple by cost band so far: under 400 about 2.8x, 400 to 800 about 4x, 800 to 1500 about 3.3x, 1500 to 2500 about 2.5x, over 2500 about 2.1x. Prices end in 50 or 00, sometimes 25, 75, 95.
1. From his new pairs, recompute the pattern per item type and show it in 5 lines.
2. Suggest a price per new item, rounded to his endings. Show cost, suggestion, multiple.
3. Flag items where the suggestion differs a lot from the prices he typed.
4. Never apply automatically: AJ confirms. Margin is judged on the selling price.
5. Items he leaves blank stay blank (pen later). Append what he taught to AJ_LEARNED.md the same day.
