# Owner's world: checked facts for AJ (10 Nov 2026)
Researched by the cloud chat builder. Not legal or tax advice: confirm each money or law point with your accountant. Each line shows where it came from.

## 1. Tax lines to watch (Sri Lanka)
- VAT: register only above Rs 15 million sales in a quarter or Rs 60 million in 12 months. The planned cut to Rs 36 million was dropped. Source: Inland Revenue notice SEC/PN/VAT/2026-03, 3 Jul 2026 ("The proposed reduction in the VAT registration threshold has been abandoned").
- SSCL (2.5% levy): threshold LOWERED from 1 Jul 2026 to Rs 9 million a quarter or Rs 36 million over four quarters; apply within 15 days of crossing. Source: SSCL (Amendment) Act No. 10 of 2026, KPMG summary, Apr 2026. Rs 9 million a quarter is about Rs 100,000 a day.
- Income tax (sole owner): about Rs 1,800,000 a year personal relief, then 6% rising to 36%. Instalments 15 Aug, 15 Nov, 15 Feb, 15 May; return by 30 Nov. Source: lankantax.com (secondary, medium confidence; check the IRD chart).
- Business name: registered at the Divisional Secretariat where the shop trades. Source: lankantax.com, ds.gov.lk pages.
- Ask the accountant: (1) Am I under the SSCL line this quarter? (2) What must my receipt show? (3) Do I pay quarterly instalments this year? (4) Which records must I keep, and for how long?

## 2. Cheques (you give and receive them)
- Bills of Exchange (Amendment) Act No. 13 of 2025: a cheque returned for no funds, closed account or stopped without good reason can be an offence. Penalty: fine up to the cheque amount, up to 2 years prison, or both.
- Steps for the person holding the cheque: written demand within 90 days of the return; the drawer then has 90 days to pay; court action within 30 days after that. The cheque must have been presented within 6 months.
- Source: desaram.com law-firm summary, Oct 2025; Act text at parliament.lk. Commencement date not confirmed: ask a lawyer before relying on it.
- For the shop: never issue a cheque the Due Board says cash will not cover; keep every returned-cheque slip (it is the evidence).

## 3. Customer data (names and phone numbers)
- Personal Data Protection Act: main duties start 1 Jan 2027 (Gazette 2498/16). No small-business exemption. Marketing-message consent rules and penalties start later by separate order. Source: bizadvisor.lk (secondary, medium confidence).
- What it means: collect only what is needed, say why, keep it safe, delete when not needed, and get consent before promotional WhatsApp or SMS, with an easy opt-out.
- Our app already: login gate, owner-only screens, encrypted backup. To add before 2027: a consent tick on the customer record and a short privacy line on receipts.

## 4. WhatsApp (Layla)
- Meta pricing: service (reply) messages were free; from 1 Oct 2026 they are charged per message, except inside the 72-hour window after a customer comes from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad. Meta's own AI "Business Agent" is charged from 1 Aug 2026. Source: developers.facebook.com WhatsApp pricing page.
- Heads-up: Layla's replies may now cost money. Check the Meta billing page once.
- General-purpose AI chatbots were restricted in 2026; a shop's own customer-service assistant appears allowed (low confidence, one secondary source). Promotional messages need opt-in.

## 5. Shop operations (working rules, builder's own knowledge, no outside source)
- Reorder point = average daily sales x vendor lead-time days + safety stock (start with 1 week of sales).
- Stocktake: count 20 fast items every week instead of everything once a year; fix differences the same day.
- Margin guard: floor price = cost / (1 - minimum margin). Example: cost Rs 6,000, minimum margin 25% -> never below Rs 8,000.
- Cheque schedule: total cheques due in a week must stay under expected sales for that week minus rent, salary and set-asides.
- Slow stock: no sale in 90 days -> move to display front, then offer card, then return-to-vendor talk.

## 6. Voice-to-text (working rules, builder's own knowledge)
- Numbers go wrong most: say "rupees twelve thousand five hundred" and the app should echo "Rs 12,500, correct?" before saving money.
- Short fixed commands beat sentences: go, hold, play N, check bridge, wrap up.
- Names: pick from a list (vendor, item) instead of dictating them.
