# Accounting and Cash Rules
- Selling at or below cost is allowed when cash is urgently needed.
- Daily cost target: Rs. 18,000.
- Daily overhead = monthly total overhead divided by days in the month.
- Credit only for trusted contractors, against a cheque.
- The 15-day return rule is a soft flag only, never a hard block.
## Cheques and vendors
- Vendor credit is 1 to 3 months, normally paid by cheque.
- A vendor payable stays open until the post-dated cheque clears the bank.
- Cheques written on Saturday clear on Monday when drawn on another bank.
- Held cheques stay visible with the original date crossed out and the new date shown beside it.
## Statutory
- EPF employer 12%, ETF 3%.
