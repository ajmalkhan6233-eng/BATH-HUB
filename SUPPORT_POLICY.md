# SUPPORT POLICY

This document ships with every licensed copy of this system and defines the support terms
between the client (the business using this software) and the vendor (contact below).

## Support Tiers

| Severity | Examples | Response & Fix Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical / Security** | System down, login broken, data at risk, suspected breach | **2–3 hours** (remote-first; on-site available locally) |
| **Major bug** | A core feature (sales, expenses, reports) gives wrong results or fails | **2–3 hours** (remote-first) |
| **Minor issue / small request** | Cosmetic problems, small wording/layout changes, questions | **4–5 business days** |
| **New feature** | Anything the system does not currently do | **Separate quotation — not covered under support** |

## Terms

- Support is provided **free for the first ______ months** from handover date: ____________
- After the free period, support continues under a subscription of **______ per month**.
- New features, integrations, and custom reports are always quoted and billed separately.
- Support covers the system as delivered; modifications made by third parties may void
  support for the affected areas until reviewed.

## How to Report a Problem

1. Open **Settings → Report a Problem** inside the system for the current contact details
   and severity guide, or contact the vendor directly:
   - **Vendor:** ____________________
   - **Phone / WhatsApp:** ____________________
   - **Email:** ____________________
2. State what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
   A screenshot or photo of the screen speeds everything up.
3. For **critical** issues, call or WhatsApp directly — do not wait on email.

## Scope Notes

- Remote support is the default channel; on-site visits are available where the vendor
  is locally present.
- Fix targets are working targets, not contractual penalties, and assume remote access
  to the machine is available when needed.
